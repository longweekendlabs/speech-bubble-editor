Editor

Three focused workspaces, one familiar editor.

Make a single image or video, generate a comic page, or arrange a polished photo collage without switching apps.

Natural speech bubbles

Oval, cloud, rectangle, starburst, text-only, scrim, and caption styles with draggable tails and live styling.

Comic Maker

Generate hand-inked 4, 6, 7, or 8-panel pages, tune the composition live, and regenerate without losing loaded images.

Photo Collage

Arrange 2–9 photos with visual layout choices, multiple canvas orientations, shuffle, and reusable named presets.

Direct photo control

Drag to crop, pull toward another frame to reorder magnetically, scale from 10–500%, or fit with blurred or solid fill.

Frame-aware effects

Apply lines, blur, or pixelate to the highlighted frame and edit background, frame ink, spacing, and corners live.

Photo and video editor

Keep the original layered editor, dual and meme modes, video timeline, trim, reverse, slow-down, and full-resolution export.

Workflow

Choose the result first; tune only what matters.

Choose a workspace

Edit normally, start Comic Maker, or open Photo Collage directly—even before loading an image.

Drop in your media

Open or drag photos into frames. Comic and Collage automatically preserve and arrange them.

Arrange and style

Crop inside a frame, drag outward to reorder, then use the dedicated right pane for layout and effects.

Export

Save a full-resolution page or still image, or render normal-mode video edits with FFmpeg.
